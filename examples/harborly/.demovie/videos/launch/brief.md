## Context
Harborly helps teams plan, ship and measure every launch. The Projects board shows every project in four columns —
Planning, In progress, Review and Launched — with health (On track, At risk, Off track), progress, due date and owner
on each card. Starting a new launch plan takes one dialog: New project → project name → Create project, and the new
project opens with a launch checklist and timeline milestones. The dashboard measures velocity week by week.

## Key points
1. Every launch on one board, from Planning to Launched, with health at a glance (routes/app-projects@desktop).
2. A new project starts as a launch plan with a checklist and milestones (flows/create-project@desktop: board → dialog → created).
3. Velocity is measured week by week on the dashboard (routes/app@desktop, velocity chart).
