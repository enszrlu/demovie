## Context
Harborly's tagline is "Plan, ship and measure every launch." This video sets it as kinetic type on the brand blue,
then gives each verb one real screen: a project's launch plan, the Projects board, and Reports.

## Key points
1. Plan — every project has a Launch checklist and a Timeline of milestones (the Q3 Launch project page).
2. Ship — the Projects board shows every project, from Planning to Launched.
3. Measure — Reports chart velocity and cycle time.
